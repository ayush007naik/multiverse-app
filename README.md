## 📱 About the Project

**Multiverse City** is a local commerce and services platform designed to bring multiple everyday services together in one application.

The goal is to make it easier for users to discover local businesses and access useful services through a single digital platform.

Built as a startup project by **Ayush Naik**, Multiverse City focuses on simplifying local discovery and connecting customers with local service providers.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛍️ Local Shopping
- Discover local shops and businesses.
- Browse products and available categories.
- Explore everyday shopping options.

### 🚚 Delivery Services
- Support for local delivery workflows.
- Delivery partner coordination.
- Delivery arrangements based on order type and vendor requirements.

### 🚖 Cab Services
- Planned categories for bike, auto, mini cab, sedan and SUV services.

### 📅 Appointments
- Doctor appointments
- Dentist appointments
- Salon bookings
- Car service appointments
- Diagnostic lab appointments

### 🏠 Real Estate
- Property discovery and real estate listings.

### 🎬 Entertainment
- Theatre discovery and entertainment information.

*Feature availability may vary. Some modules may still be under development.*
